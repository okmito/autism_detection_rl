Overview

Important: NeuroBridge is an explainable, adaptive screening and support research prototype. It provides screening/referral-research estimates and non-diagnostic support ideas only — it does not confirm or rule out autism and is not a substitute for professional assessment. Some research results shown are based on questionnaire-derived/circular labels and have known limitations.
